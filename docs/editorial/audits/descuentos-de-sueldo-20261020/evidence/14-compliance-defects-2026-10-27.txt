D9 — product / media — union fee applicability
Local: src/data/blog/descuentos-de-sueldo.md:108 says “si estás afiliado a un sindicato” and frames the ordinary fee as applicable to affiliates.
BCN Code Art. 322: a non-affiliated worker who accepts an agreed extension of benefits must undertake to pay all or part of ordinary dues as fixed by the agreement.
BCN Code Art. 323: a worker who changes union or leaves the union remains subject to the union’s collective instrument and owes the full ordinary monthly fee for the duration of that instrument.
Official extracts: evidence/11-compliance-primary-sources-2026-10-27.txt [S1, S3].
Impact: an employee may wrongly treat these dues as unlawful because they are not currently affiliated or because they resigned.

D10 — product / media — AFC exceptions
Local: descontos-de-sueldo.md:104 states employee 0.6% for indefinite contracts and only generally mentions groups such as pensioners; primer-empleo...md:110 says 0.6% is fixed/inamovible; primer-empleo...md:189 says the insurance is mandatory for every worker with a current employment contract; liquidacion...md:105 says 0.6% only when indefinite.
BCN Law 19.728 Arts. 2 and 5: apprentices and under-18 workers are excluded; pensioners have specified exceptions; the worker’s 0.6% applies to indefinite contracts except household employees; household workers’ full 3.0% contribution is employer-paid regardless of contract duration.
Official extracts: evidence/11-compliance-primary-sources-2026-10-27.txt [S5, S6].
Impact: a household worker on an indefinite contract could be told that 0.6% must be deducted; related pages state broader mandatory coverage than the law.

D12 — product / media — missing safeguard for time-not-worked deductions
Local: descontos-de-sueldo.md:138 says an employer can deduct the corresponding time not worked and that a late arrival is not a full-day absence; it does not state that the calculation must be checked at the end of the week.
DT ORD. N°1578, 27-mar-2018: “la sola circunstancia de que el dependiente no cumpla en su integridad su jornada diaria, no autoriza al empleador para proceder de inmediato al descuento ... debiendo verificarse al término de la respectiva semana ... si aquél ha incumplido la jornada laboral convenida por causas que le sean imputables.”
DT ORD. N°2423, 05-jun-2017 supports deduction only for time not worked when the agreed shift was not completed for a cause attributable to the worker.
DT guidance confirms the general weekly limit became 42 hours on 26-apr-2026. ORD. 1578’s excerpt still refers to the former 45-hour limit.
Official extracts: evidence/11-compliance-primary-sources-2026-10-27.txt [S2].
Impact: without the week-end check, an employee could infer that any late arrival by itself warrants an immediate deduction. A current interpretation should confirm the rule under the current weekly limit.
