**Goal**  
Strengthen Monedario's SEO foundations so existing content and calculators can capture search demand more efficiently.

**Strategic rationale**  
Monedario already has meaningful content breadth, calculator utility, and trust signals. The immediate bottleneck is not lack of assets, but weak packaging of those assets into a clear search-intent architecture. **Key metrics to monitor**
- Total impressions and clicks in Google Search Console.
- Non-brand clicks for priority queries in Chile.
- Homepage CTR from search.

## Phase 2 - Authority Building

**Goal**  
Turn Monedario's existing article and calculator inventory into defensible topic clusters around the financial decisions Chilean users actually search for.

**Strategic rationale**  
Once the acquisition layer is in place, growth will depend on building clear topical authority. Monedario should win a focused set of recurring decisions first: sueldo, AFP/APV, crédito, cesantía, UF, impuestos, and presupuesto. The objective is to create cluster hubs, tighten internal linking, and refresh high-intent assets.

**Expected outcomes**
- Stronger topical authority around core Chilean personal finance decisions.
- Higher internal-link density between guides, calculators, glossary, and legal explainers.
- Better user navigation from discovery page to decision-support page.
- Improved freshness and completeness on high-intent evergreen content.

**Key metrics to monitor**
- Query breadth and page-level impressions by cluster.
- Average position for cluster hub pages.
- Internal clicks from articles to calculators.
- Organic sessions landing on cluster hubs.
- Scroll depth and engagement on refreshed high-intent pages.

## Horizon 1 — Regulatory Accuracy and Behavioral Retention

> Derived from deep research synthesis (2026-04-11). MB-038 is a factual correction and does not require founder approval. All others are IDEA-stage.

**Goal**  
Correct legally-mandated content accuracy issues, add behavioral retention infrastructure, and open two first-mover tool opportunities before competitors do.

**Strategic rationale**  
AI Overview erosion of CTR on informational queries is already measurable. The defensive response is (a) tools that require interactivity AI cannot replace, and (b) regulatory freshness that makes Monedario the first reliable source when laws change. Both levers require acting before search demand crystallizes around other players.

**Expected outcomes**

- Calculadora sueldo-líquido accurate under Ley 40h.
- Return-visit rate measurable via localStorage restore events.
- FES/CAE simulator live before first enrollment window.
- SOAP comparador live before March renewal peak (validates affiliate model).
- Calculator result blocks surface loss-framing alongside the answer.

**Key metrics to monitor**
- Calculator sessions with localStorage restore event (return rate proxy).
- FES simulator sessions and CTA click-through in first 30 days.
- SOAP comparador affiliate click-through and CPA revenue.
- Calculator result-block CTA click-through before and after MB-042 framing.
